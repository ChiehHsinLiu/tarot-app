# Proposal

## Why

The owner wants a second, more playful look for tarot readings: a dachshund deck, where every one of the 78 cards is a scene starring dachshunds. The card meanings stay the same, so the new deck changes only how readings look and is fun to choose.

## What Changes

- Add a **Dachshund** tarot deck: the same 78 cards, names, meanings and yes/no verdicts as the current deck, but every card (all 22 major arcana, the 16 court cards, and the 40 Ace to Ten cards) has its own original dachshund drawing. The Ace to Ten drawings still show their suit's symbol as many times as the card's number, so they can be counted.
- Show a **"Choose a deck"** choice on the public tarot screen with **Classic** (today's deck, the default) and **Dachshund**. The face-down preview, the reading and the reveal work the same for both. In the local dev app, Lenormand stays a third option in the same choice.
- Dachshund faces keep the cream face, the five suit colour families, the name and the numeral. All decks keep the shared night-sky card back.
- **Share links remember the deck**: a Dachshund reading's link adds `d=dachshund`, and opening it shows the Dachshund pictures. Classic links don't change, so every link already shared still works and still opens in Classic.
- The deck choice stays the same after Draw and New reading, and resets to Classic when the tarot screen is entered from elsewhere, like the spread and the question.
- The drawings are made in batches, and the owner approves each batch on a contact sheet. Nothing is pushed until all 78 are approved.

Not changing: card text, spreads, the Copy for Claude prompt (meanings are identical), the animal oracle, and the Lenormand deck.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities
- `tarot-deck-styles`: choosing between the tarot decks' art styles on the tarot screen (Classic and Dachshund), the Dachshund drawings and their rules, and how the chosen style carries into readings and share links.

### Modified Capabilities
- `lenormand-readings`: the deck choice is no longer dev-only. The public site now shows "Choose a deck" with Classic and Dachshund, while Lenormand stays dev-only and stays out of the public bundle. The dev choice's "Tarot" option is now called "Classic".
- `reading-share-links`: the link format gains an optional `d=dachshund` parameter. Links without it are Classic.

## Impact

- **Data and art:** 78 new SVG drawings in `src/assets/dachshund/`, generated by new draw scripts in `scripts/dachshund-art/` (`npm run draw:dachshund`), plus a contact-sheet script for owner review.
- **Code:** `src/components/DeckPicker.tsx` (now public, with three ids), `Composer.tsx`, `CardFace.tsx` (picks the drawing by style), `ReadingView.tsx` / `SpreadPreview.tsx` as needed, `src/screens/tarot-state.ts` and `TarotScreens.tsx` (the reading carries its style), `src/lib/share.ts` (encode and decode `d`), a new `src/lib/dachshund-art.ts`, and `src/lib/art-format.ts` (a size limit for the denser scenes).
- **Tests:** unit tests for the art format and coverage, and for share encode/decode, plus e2e checks for the picker, a Dachshund reading, and a Dachshund share link. The tarot screen's file grows by the drawings (estimated at roughly 200 KB raw, about 50 KB compressed). The home page's first load does not change.
